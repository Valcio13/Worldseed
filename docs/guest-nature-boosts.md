# Guest Nature Boosts

These purchases are guest-only. They do not affect verified V1/V2 runs, PP/CP rewards, or verifier commands.

| Purchase | Nature cost | Effect | Limit and reset |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Nature Surge | 1,500 | Multiplies Nature production by 4 for 30 seconds. It stacks with other temporary boosts. Buying it again while active charges again and refreshes it to 30 seconds. | No per-run limit; the Nature cost applies each time. |
| Fertile Soil | 1,000 × next level (1,000–5,000) | Adds 10% to total Nature production per level, up to +50%. The bonus is additive and applies to every habitat. | Five levels per run; all levels reset on Rebirth. |
| Time Bloom | 20 seconds of current total production | Instantly adds 30 seconds of current total production to Nature. Nature spent is excluded from lifetime/run totals; the generated 30-second reward is included. | Once per run; use resets on Rebirth. |

Existing Sunny Season and Deep Roots retain their current costs, multipliers, and durations. Temporary effects expire at the exact elapsed time during active ticks and offline recovery.

The new purchases are deliberately kept out of Verified Mode until Codex 1 defines and implements canonical support.
